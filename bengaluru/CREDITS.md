# Bengaluru

`bengaluru.pmtiles` holds the wards, the 150 m grid and every layer below; `cog/` holds the rasters
worth seeing at 30 m. Each layer keeps the licence of what it is built from. A layer under a
share-alike licence (ODbL, CC BY-SA) must be shared on the same terms if you change it, and a
non-commercial one (NC) cannot be used commercially.

Wards: Municipal Spatial Data by DataMeet India community (CC BY 4.0), 2022 delimitation (BBMP, 243 wards, from KSRSAC), https://raw.githubusercontent.com/datameet/Municipal_Spatial_Data/0c3a2e3dd2e87c514817378d6c73a9dd2ffb8f69/Bangalore/BBMP.geojson
